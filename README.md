# How a New York City public school teacher gets hired and fired

Two sourced process flowcharts:

1. **Getting hired** — from New York State certification (traditional and alternative routes) through the TeachNYC application, school-based hiring and nomination, city fingerprinting, onboarding and the four-year probationary appointment.
2. **Getting fired** — the three distinct paths the job can end: discontinuance of a probationary (untenured) teacher, the Education Law §3020-a / §3020-b process for a tenured teacher, and seniority-based layoffs under §2588.

Every step is annotated with **how long it takes**, **who is involved** and **the law or rule it hinges on**, with inline citations to primary law and official sources.

Single self-contained `index.html` — no build, no dependencies.

Compiled June 2026 from public law and official sources. Not legal advice.
