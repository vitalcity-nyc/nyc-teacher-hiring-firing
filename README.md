The June 2026 standalone version had errors, found on September 11, 2026:

- It showed a separate ineffective-ratings lane under Education Law §3020-b. That section was repealed by Chapter 143 of the Laws of 2024 (S9054/A9849).
- It cited Panel for Educational Policy bylaws §4.3.2 for probationary review. That rule is now §13.4.2 (approved March 12, 2025).
- It said §3020-a powers are "delegated" to the chancellor. Education Law §2590-h(38) and (38-a) give them to the chancellor directly.
- It described hearing officers as picked from an American Arbitration Association list in about 15 days. In New York City they are assigned jointly by the city and the United Federation of Teachers from standing panels (2023 memorandum of agreement).
- It cited §3004-c (denial of certification) for fingerprinting and §2590-i for teacher selection. The correct citations are §§3004-b and 3035 for fingerprinting, §2590-h(20) with Chancellor's Regulation C-105 for the city check and §2590-j(4)(a) for appointment.
- It gave clocks with no source: a 60-day nomination expiry, about 20 weeks to evaluate salary steps and 2 to 6 weeks for exam scores.
- It did not name the case behind the probationary bad-faith standard: Matter of Frasier v Board of Educ., 71 NY2d 763 (1988).

The consolidated chart's source base, with a verbatim quote for every claim, is `research/teacher.json` in the nyc-public-jobs-hire-fire repo. The old page stays in this repo's git history (commit 05d9d34).
